# Wikipedia's role in the LLM era (philosophy and technology)

**Current draft: `essay_full_draft_v3.md` (5 sections, about 4,100 words, focused on Wikipedia in the LLM era; see `V2_CHANGES.md`). Earlier: v2 (`essay_full_draft_v2.md`), nine-section v1 (`essay_full_draft.md`).**

Background work for an argumentative essay on two claims: that Wikipedia's standing as a "source of truth" in the era of large language models is relative to its alternatives, and that AI reuse of Wikipedia can comply with its licence while breaching an implicit commons social contract.

- `literature_review.md`: narrative review, broad scope, with reference list and a list of what must be read in full before drafting.
- `outline.md`: proposed research question, thesis and section plan, with evidence status. Not yet confirmed by the author.
- `annotated_bibliography/`: the six search threads, one file per thread. Each entry has a citation, a verification line (what was opened), an annotation limited to what was seen, and its bearing on the two claims. First-person remarks in these files are search notes about what could and could not be read.

Reading status: most academic sources were assessed from abstracts or catalogue records. Check the verification line of an entry before quoting it.
