# 14. Pāli suttas to read before citing

Texts are on SuttaCentral (suttacentral.net) in Thích Minh Châu's Vietnamese translation and Bhikkhu Sujato's English translation. The sites phatphapungdung.com and thuvienhoasen.org were suggested by the author but returned a Cloudflare challenge (HTTP 403) to the research tools and were not used; budsas.org answered but was not needed since SuttaCentral carries Thích Minh Châu's translation.
