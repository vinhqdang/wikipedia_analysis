## 4. Remaining weaknesses and fixes

**N1. "Impermanence" still headlines a paper that disclaims it as a premise.** Severity: Minor | Evidence Anchor: text: Title "Conditioned norms: impermanence and the fate of Wikipedia's authority" and 6.5 "Hume's conventionalism could carry the argument alone" | Confidence: 4.
Fix: either drop "impermanence" from the title and keywords, or add one case where the Buddhist notion does work the Humean one does not (for instance the claim that the norm's passing is not a failure). P&T readers are likelier to read the lens as ornament than to credit it.

**N2. Two locator errors and one gloss that overstates.** Severity: Minor | Evidence Anchor: text: "(Hume, 1739-1740/2003, book III, part II, section II)" and "(cf. Arnold, n.d., section 2.a)" | Confidence: 5 (both checked against primary text).
Fix: cite III.ii.i for the "arises artificially" clause; change Arnold to section 2.c; soften "most philosophers hold ... under the description of benefiting" (Manela section 2).

**N3. "Lapsing" is still asserted in the abstract and conclusion, while 5.3 says editor attitudes are unmeasured.** Severity: Minor | Evidence Anchor: text: Abstract "Some norms are lapsing, some are being rewritten by the community" versus 5.3 "has not been measured systematically" | Confidence: 4.
Fix: phrase the abstract as "some norms appear to be lapsing", or name which norms have documented lapse (the older credibility norm, Section 2.2) and which are conjectured (the reciprocity norm).

**N4. Appendix A and the Stack Overflow figures remain non-reproducible.** Severity: Minor | Evidence Anchor: text: Appendix A "queries and results are kept with the project files" and 4.3 "(author's retrieval from the Stack Exchange data interface, October 2026)" | Confidence: 4.
Fix: deposit the queries and result tables in an open repository and cite the DOI or URL. P&T is a conceptual venue: a journal editor will ask why a 2018 culture-gap measure and a 2026 proxy sit in an essay about norms; either state the argumentative role in one sentence or move to a supplement. Explain the October 2025 end-point for the Stack Overflow series.

**N5. The 7.1 premise that readers benefit from a dispute record is asserted.** Severity: Minor to Major for the claim's strength (I rate Minor because the essay marks the proposal as its own) | Evidence Anchor: text: "so that a reader of a generated answer can follow a claim back to its source and to the dispute about it" | Confidence: 3 (empirical HCI sits at the edge of my competence).
Fix: add one sentence citing work on whether readers use talk pages or revision histories (Magnus 2009 argues readers need such cues but gives no usage data), or flag that the premise is untested alongside the three limits in Section 8. Add a two-line worked example (a disputed claim, what travels, what the reader sees).

**N6. Foundation traffic explanation presented in part.** Severity: Minor | Evidence Anchor: text: Section 4.2 "attributes this to search engines 'providing answers directly to searchers, often based on Wikipedia content'" | Confidence: 3.
Fix: add "and notes revised bot detection and shifts toward social video among younger users" with a page anchor.

**N7. Equivocation between social norms and formal policy rules.** Severity: Minor | Evidence Anchor: text: 2.3 "'Ignore all rules' ... generated a page of over 500 words" next to the Bicchieri definition of 2.1 | Confidence: 3.
Fix: one sentence saying that Wikipedia policies are written rules and that the essay treats them as the codified face of social norms, so the Bicchieri account applies to their uptake and not to the policy text.

**N8. Gallert & van der Velden reference remains provisional.** Severity: Minor | Evidence Anchor: text: reference "(Unedited preliminary version; final publication details unconfirmed.)" | Confidence: 5.
Fix: locate the published version or move the claim into a hedged clause.

**N9. Repetition of three points.** Severity: Minor | Confidence: 5.
Fix: say once that a citation link does not carry the dispute record (Section 3) and refer back; trim the "Three limits" paragraph where it repeats 6.5.

**N10. Housekeeping.** Remove orphan 2025e; reorder 2025a/b; update "preprint" for Hora & Robbes in 4.4; Section 5.4 is long; consider splitting into the tacit-to-explicit claim and the critical-mass claim.
