## Statements that are the essay's own, not the literature's

- "Source of truth" can mean accuracy, provenance or deference. (Analytic distinction.)
- Credibility is a three-place relation. (Conceptual claim; no study tests it.)
- Provenance, contestability and answerability, together "auditability". (Working vocabulary, flagged in the text.)
- Gaps in the published record become gaps in the encyclopedia. (Inference from the rules and the studies above.)
